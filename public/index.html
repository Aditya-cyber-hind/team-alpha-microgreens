🔬 The Science Behind It

💧 What is Hydroponics?

Growing plants without soil! Plants get nutrients directly from water mixed with minerals. It saves 90% more water than traditional farming!

🌱 What are Microgreens?

Baby plants harvested after 7-14 days! They contain up to 40x more nutrients than mature vegetables. Perfect for quick, healthy food!

⚡ Why It's Awesome

No soil = no mess! Plants grow 30-50% faster in hydroponics. Perfect for urban areas and even space missions! 🚀
